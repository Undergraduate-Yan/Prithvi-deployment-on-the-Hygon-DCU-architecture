## Coverage boundaries

- The deployment study uses fixed task checkpoints. Retraining is not a substitute for obtaining the selected checkpoints, and may not reproduce their byte identities or exact predictions.
- Trial orchestration, recovery, telemetry and aggregation are included for the reported system-measurement families. Original remote login, transfer and scheduler administration are not required research implementations and are not distributed.
- Inference inputs, weights, graph/cache payloads, historical intermediate tensors, and some original construction reports remain external. The full-precision 1536-record block campaign is not reconstructed from its aggregate table.
- Original failed exploratory attempts remain described by the retained records. The provided probe builders support the relevant graph-construction mechanisms; exact historical replay requires each attempt's original inputs and report. Resource failures need not occur identically on another host.
- All final publication figure layouts are not regenerated by this package. Numerical source tables and the diagnostic plotting implementation support scientific examination; visual similarity is not numerical validation.
- Some imported scientific modules retain historical schema names and original defensive checks. These are compatibility requirements. They are not evidence that a new execution was prospectively frozen or previously unseen.
- Work Buddy should assign PASS/FAIL/BLOCKED/NOT_RUN separately to every ID. Do not mark the whole inventory complete because offline analysis succeeds.
