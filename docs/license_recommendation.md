# Proposed code license

Apache License 2.0 is the recommended license for study-authored software, subject to authorization by the relevant rights holders. The active repository license remains the permissions notice in `LICENSE` until that decision is made.

The license allows reuse, modification and redistribution, including commercial use, subject to its conditions. It includes an explicit contributor patent grant, retention of relevant notices, notice requirements for modified files and a warranty disclaimer. It does not require downstream users to publish all their modifications.

Prithvi-EO-2.0-300M and TerraTorch identify Apache-2.0 as their license. Alignment with those dependencies simplifies attribution, but does not establish that every other dependency, dataset, trained weight or vendor component can be redistributed under the same terms.

If adopted, apply the license only to material the project has authority to license. Retain third-party notices, keep excluded data/model/vendor payloads under their respective terms, and retain `CITATION.cff` as a scholarly citation request. The Apache license does not itself guarantee academic citation or prohibit commercial use.
